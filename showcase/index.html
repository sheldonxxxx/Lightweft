Behind the photographs

From a brief
to finished photographs.

GPT-6 Astra searched my Immich library for galaxy photographs and chose these two. It directed the edits in RapidRAW, reviewing the rendered results along the way.

The edits adjusted exposure, colour and local light. Denoising cleaned up the sky, with some softening of faint detail. Each comparison uses the same photograph and composition.

Lightweft gives AI agents artistic direction and a shared app for reviewing edits with you. Use it with the editor you choose.

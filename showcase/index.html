Explore your own style

A shared palette.
A fresh decision for every photograph.

Style Builder develops a look through real comparisons. These four examples show saved film-inspired presets made in RapidRAW. Choose a direction, test it across different scenes, and keep the relationships that make each photograph work.

  1. 01

    Read the photograph

    Start with its light, subject and atmosphere. Prepare a convincing base for that scene.

  2. 02

    Explore a palette

    Render alternatives against the same base. Compare what each look adds and what it gives up.

  3. 03

    Review a collection

    Test across changing light and complete outings. Save the look that holds together, then finish each photograph.

These are demonstrations of a personal style study; the four presets are not bundled with the skill.

Behind the photographs

From a brief
to finished photographs.

GPT-6 Astra searched my Immich library for galaxy photographs and chose these two. It directed the edits in RapidRAW, reviewing the rendered results along the way.

The edits adjusted exposure, colour and local light. Denoising cleaned up the sky, with some softening of faint detail. Each comparison uses the same photograph and composition.

Lightweft gives AI agents artistic direction and a shared app for reviewing edits with you. Use it with the editor you choose.

Behind the photographs

From the backlog
to a finished photograph.

For this galaxy series, I wanted an impactful edit. GPT-6 Astra searched my Immich library for galaxy photographs, chose these two, and directed the edits in RapidRAW: exposure, colour, local light and denoising, with rendered results reviewed along the way.

The before is an unadjusted RAW rendering. The after uses the same photograph and composition. No sky was generated or replaced.

Woodland Mirror’s house and reflection received a further gentle lift following photographer feedback. Denoising makes the sky cleaner, with some softening of faint detail.
